### 4. Copyright Violations
**Status:** Resolved - Original violation identified and fixed.

**Original Issue:**
- **File:** `Industry-Hybrid-Standard.md`
- **Content:** Full reproduction of the research paper "Industry 4.0 vs. Industry 5.0: Co-existence, Transition, or a Hybrid" by Mariia Golovianko et al.
- **License:** CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (prohibits commercial use)
- **Violation:** Used in commercial context without permission.

**Resolution:**
- Removed the copyrighted content.
- Replaced with original Keplar Flow framework summary.
- No further copyright risks in this file.

**Other Content:**
- All other documentation remains original and properly licensed.
