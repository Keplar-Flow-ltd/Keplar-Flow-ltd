# NotebookLM Source Scrubbing and Sanitization Protocol

## Objective
Conduct a comprehensive ethical, legal, and compliance audit of all sources in this NotebookLM workspace. Identify any materials that may violate copyright, trademark, patent, or ethical standards. Extract only the non-violating portions and generate sanitized versions of sources containing violations to ensure full compliance with intellectual property laws and ethical guidelines.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Phase 1: Source Inventory and Analysis
1. **List All Sources:** Compile a complete inventory of all documents, files, web pages, PDFs, and other materials currently attached or referenced in this workspace.

2. **Categorize Sources:**
   - **Public Domain:** Works with expired copyrights (generally pre-1929 in the US, or explicitly public domain).
   - **Licensed/Open Source:** Materials under permissive licenses (MIT, Apache, CC BY, etc.) that allow commercial use.
   - **Fair Use Eligible:** Limited excerpts for criticism, commentary, education, or research.
   - **Original Content:** Materials created by the workspace owner or with explicit permission.
   - **Potentially Violating:** Any content that may infringe on copyrights, trademarks, or patents.

3. **Ethical Assessment:**
   - Check for content that promotes harm, bias, discrimination, or unethical practices.
   - Ensure all AI-generated or synthesized content maintains ethical standards.
   - Verify compliance with data privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).

### Phase 2: Violation Detection
For each source, perform the following checks:

1. **Copyright Analysis:**
   - Is the work still under copyright protection?
   - Does the license allow commercial use and modification?
   - Is the use within fair use guidelines (purpose, nature, amount, effect)?
   - Are there any Creative Commons restrictions (NC, ND, SA)?

2. **Trademark Review:**
   - Does the content use third-party trademarks without permission?
   - Are brand names, logos, or slogans used in a way that could cause confusion?

3. **Patent Examination:**
   - Does the content describe patented processes, inventions, or technologies?
   - Is the description generic enough to avoid infringement?

4. **Ethical Compliance:**
   - Does the content align with principles of decent work and human rights?
   - Are there any privacy or data protection concerns?

### Phase 3: Sanitization Process
For sources identified with violations:

1. **Extract Safe Portions:**
   - Identify and isolate sections that are:
     - Public domain
     - Properly licensed
     - Fair use eligible
     - Original/factual information not protected by IP
   - Remove or redact violating content.

2. **Generate Sanitized Copies:**
   - Create new, clean versions of violating sources containing only the extracted safe portions.
   - Clearly label sanitized versions with:
     - Original source attribution
     - Date of sanitization
     - Compliance status
     - Removed content summary

3. **Document Removals:**
   - Maintain a log of what was removed and why.
   - Note any alternative sources for similar information.

### Phase 4: Workspace Rebuild
1. **Replace Violating Sources:**
   - Remove original violating sources from the workspace.
   - Attach the sanitized versions in their place.

2. **Update Synthesized Content:**
   - Regenerate any notes, summaries, or insights that were based on violating materials.
   - Ensure all outputs are based only on compliant sources.

3. **Compliance Verification:**
   - Re-run the analysis on the sanitized workspace.
   - Confirm no violations remain.

### Phase 5: Documentation and Reporting
1. **Generate Compliance Report:**
   - List all sources and their compliance status.
   - Detail violations found and actions taken.
   - Provide recommendations for future source additions.

2. **Ethical Guidelines for Future Use:**
   - Establish rules for adding new sources.
   - Require pre-attachment compliance checks.

## Output Requirements
- **Sanitized Source Files:** Generate downloadable, sanitized versions of any violating sources.
- **Compliance Report:** A comprehensive document detailing the audit process and findings.
- **Workspace Status:** Clear indication of whether the workspace is now fully compliant.

## Important Notes
- This process prioritizes ethical compliance and legal safety over completeness.
- When in doubt about a source's status, err on the side of caution and remove/sanitize.
- Consult legal experts for complex cases involving international copyright or emerging technologies.
- This protocol aligns with principles of responsible AI use and intellectual property respect.

## Execution
Begin the scrubbing process immediately and provide real-time updates on progress. Generate all sanitized files and the compliance report as outputs.
